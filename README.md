# Lite Video Downloader (YT, FB, X, IG, TikTok)

## How to Get Your APK in 2 Minutes (Without Installing Anything)
1. Create a free account at [GitHub.com](https://github.com) and create a new **Repository**.
2. Click **Add file -> Upload files**, extract this ZIP folder, and drag all the extracted folders/files into your repository. Click **Commit changes**.
3. Click the **Actions** tab at the top of your GitHub repository.
4. Click on the running workflow (**Build Lite APK**). Wait ~90 seconds for it to finish.
5. Scroll down to **Artifacts** and click **LiteDownloader-APK** to download your ready-to-install `.apk` file!
